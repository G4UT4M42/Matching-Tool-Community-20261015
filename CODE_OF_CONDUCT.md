# Contributor conduct

Contributors must discuss code, behavior, security and financial correctness respectfully and professionally. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, doxxing, publication of private bank or customer records, and intentional credential disclosure are prohibited.

Use synthetic reproductions when reporting defects. Never put real invoices, account numbers, customer identities, access keys, browser profiles or tax returns into public issues or patches.

Maintainers may moderate issues and contributions that violate these requirements. Use the private vulnerability-reporting procedure in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for security-sensitive reports.
